# Part 2: Device Identity and Fingerprints

> Identity is "who I am"; a fingerprint is "whether I am who I think I am."

This part breaks down the device identity system:

- Which identifiers are generated by the client, and which are issued by the server?
- What do static fingerprints and dynamic collection each observe?
- Through which encrypted carriers are the collection results reported?
- How does the server use four states to express risk-control decisions?
