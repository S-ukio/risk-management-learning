This chapter introduces no new algorithms; it does three things:

1. review the experimental data given at the end of the source;
2. list the source gaps, unclear semantics, and questions that require measurement;
3. define research boundaries and ethical requirements.

## Why a separate chapter

The source is information-dense, but it still has many "blank spots":

- 2.3's "three token entry points" is followed by nothing;
- 3.1's list of clock values is missing;
- 4.3's two "branch composition" passages are not followed by tables;
- at the end of 4.3, "eight branches" conflicts in count with the earlier "16 branches".

These are not minor typesetting issues. They determine whether you can actually reproduce the result, and they determine what this note should fill in next.
